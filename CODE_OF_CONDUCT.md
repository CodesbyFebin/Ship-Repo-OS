# Code of conduct

Everyone participating in ShipOS issues, pull requests, and project discussions is expected to treat others with respect.

- Discuss ideas and evidence without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and ask before sharing someone else's contact information or private messages.
- Welcome questions and contributions from people with different experience levels.
- Harassment, threats, discrimination, and publishing private information are unacceptable.

Report conduct concerns to a repository maintainer through a private contact channel. Include links and relevant context. Maintainers may remove harmful content, limit participation, or block accounts based on the behavior and its impact. GitHub's abuse reporting tools are available when a project contact is unavailable.
